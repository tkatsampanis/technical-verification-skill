### Core Rule (Post-Section 21)
> Technical rigor means verifying the things that can make the answer wrong.
> Do not perform additional technical work merely because more analysis is possible.
> Use the minimum technical analysis necessary to establish a reliable result.

## How This Skill Works

**Import this skill via Hermes Agent** when your answer materially depends on:
- Equations and mathematical models
- Numerical calculations or simulations  
- Units, dimensions, and conversions
- Assumptions and boundary conditions
- Code-dependent technical work

**Verification depth is proportionate:**
- Simple queries: Check authoritative definitions
- Medium queries: Full input/unit/model/calculation verification
- Substantial tasks: Maintain explicit state table (Section 19), track all questions/evidence/assumptions

**Completion condition:** All required technical questions are answered sufficiently — not when every potentially interesting detail has been examined.
